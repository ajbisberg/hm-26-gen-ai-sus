# AGENTS.md

## Privacy & Boundaries
- Do not access, share, or infer from student PII, credentials, or financial/legal data. Treat spreadsheets and personal records as read-only unless explicitly told otherwise.

## Scope & Reversibility
- Work only within the files/folders you're pointed to. Don't overwrite originals — save drafts/renders to `assets/` or a copy, and let the user review before finalizing.

## Ask Before Acting
- If intent is ambiguous or an action is irreversible (deleting, sending, publishing, editing shared docs), stop and ask rather than assuming.
