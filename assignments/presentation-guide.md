# Final Pitch — Presentation Guide

**NextGen Campus Challenge · Thursday, July 23**

Audience: professors, staff, and invited guests.

---

## 1. Format

- **Pitch:** 20 minutes
- **Q&A:** 5 minutes, questions held until the pitch ends
- **Slides:** ~12-15 core slides, possibly appendix slides for Q&A 
- **Demo:** 90 seconds maximum, screenshot fallback directly after it in the deck
- **Speakers:** everyone speaks; fluent handoff
- **Data:** anonymize interviewee data

Presentation time: 1:30 PM Thu July 23

---

## 2. Content checklist

Core items (★) are most important — roughly a minute each. Unstarred items must exist but may be brief or sit in the appendix. **A thin slide beats a missing slide.**

1. Startup name, tagline, vision — 30 seconds
2. ★ Problem statement from student interviews, with real-vs-AI-role-play disclosure
3. User personas
4. Physical space concept
5. ★ Sustainable AI infrastructure concept, including the compute decision matrix
6. ★ Agentic AI workflow diagram
7. Data-flow and minimization concept
8. ★ Privacy, evaluation, and ethics concept
9. ★ Sustainability scorecard — human dimensions, and at least one measured energy row
10. Market and operating model — one slide, bottom-up
11. Implementation roadmap
12. Optional prototype, mockup, storyboard, or AI demo
13. ★ Final funding ask — what support the startup needs from HM

Two items get skipped most often:

- **Market and operating model** 
- **Roadmap and ask** — *"We ask HM for [resources] to run [phase 1] for [duration], measured by [evaluation plan]. If we hit [criteria], we propose phase 2."*

## 3. Assessment criteria

Seven criteria, each rated **above expectations / meets expectations / below expectations**. 

### Human-centered problem
*Do I believe real students need this?*

- **Above** — a specific person's specific pain, with counts from your own fieldwork; what they do today and why it fails; real vs. simulated insights disclosed unprompted
- **Meets** — a plausible, interview-supported problem, stated at theme level
- **Below** — an industry trend used as a problem, or claimed validation with nothing behind it

### Sustainable compute strategy
*Did they compare options convincingly?*

- **Above** — on-prem / shared cluster / hyperscaler compared on *your* numbers: energy per agentic task scaled to your user volume, a grid sensitivity check with the best-vs-worst multiplier, data sensitivity classified, hybrid split considered, trade-off quantified in CO₂e
- **Meets** — a decision matrix and a defended choice, but numbers borrowed rather than derived, or the trade-off argued qualitatively
- **Below** — a deployment choice with no comparison, or worked-example numbers reused as your own

### Agentic AI concept
*Useful, understandable, plausible?*

- **Above** — input → decision → tools/data → output, with human approval and failure modes visible; obvious what the agent does that a booking app could not; someone technical could build a v0 from the slide
- **Meets** — understandable and useful agents, but the diagram hides where the model actually decides, or review and failure handling surface only under questioning
- **Below** — "AI-powered" as a label; boxes and arrows that could describe any system

### Space & interaction design
*Clear, creative, realistic?*

- **Above** — a nameable room, changed in ways a facilities manager would call buildable; one day-in-the-life path from need to outcome; reuse and inclusion as decisions, not adjectives
- **Meets** — a coherent space concept, but its link to the AI system is asserted rather than shown
- **Below** — a render standing in for a concept; a room that could be any room

### Sustainability & circularity
*Measured, or vibes?*

- **Above** — at least one row you measured yourself (mini-lab), read as an energy label — kWh and g CO₂e per 1,000 requests, % routed to the small model — against a stated baseline; every other row labeled measured or estimated; circularity and human dimensions addressed
- **Meets** — a complete, well-cited scorecard, but no measurement of your own, or estimates not distinguished from measurements
- **Below** — sustainability as a value claim; unsourced numbers

### Privacy, ethics & governance
*Would I opt in?*

- **Above** — data-flow map and risk table visibly drive the design: what is collected, why, how long, who sees it, what is minimized away; opt-out exists and the room still works without it; someone is accountable when the agent is wrong
- **Meets** — privacy addressed as compliance language rather than design consequence; opt-out unclear
- **Below** — "data is anonymized and secure," full stop; sensors with no purpose limitation

### Pitch quality & prototype
*Convincing, visual, startup-like?*

- **Above** — one story (problem → vision → how → proof → ask) inside 10 minutes with clean handoffs; one interaction demonstrated excellently, plus the honest "does not yet" sentence; Q&A answers the question asked and concedes gaps
- **Meets** — clear and complete, but reads as a report of the work done; the demo covers more than it can show well
- **Below** — over time, unrehearsed transitions, a failed demo with no fallback
